## What it reports

A redux-saga test steps the generator with `.next()` and asserts each yielded effect in sequence. It pins how the saga is written, not what it does, and breaks on a reorder that changes no behaviour.

## What to do

Run the saga instead (`expectSaga(saga).run()`, one test per branch) and keep the effects it asserts. Do not delete the assertions.

`spirolo explain generator-walk` prints the same claim and status from your installed binary.
