## What it reports

Across the runs recorded in your run ledger, the test both passed and failed on the same tree, and the failure was the test's own, not a run that crashed as a whole. Its result depends on something other than the code: the clock, a random draw, a fixed wait, shared state, run order or load.

## What to do

Compare the failing runs' conditions with the passing ones, and make the test control what varies. Until then, `spirolo flake quarantine` adds it to `.spirolo/quarantine.json`; nothing is applied to your runner.

## What it reads

The rule reads the ledger that `spirolo flake ingest` builds under `.spirolo/runs/` from Vitest JSON or JUnit XML reports. It never runs your tests.

`spirolo explain flake-observed` prints the same claim and status from your installed binary.
